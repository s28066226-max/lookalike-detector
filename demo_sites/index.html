Demo look-alike pages

Fake pages used to demonstrate the Look-Alike Website Detector. Serve them only on localhost: python -m http.server 8001 --directory demo_sites. None of the forms send anything.

  • sbi/: fake SBI NetBanking login (form posts to another site)
  • incometax/: fake Income Tax refund page (asks for PAN and card details)
  • aadhaar/: fake Aadhaar KYC update (form sends to an e-mail address)
